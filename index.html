01 / Released Title

Crop Circles

Crop Circles is a creepy-cute 3D game developed in Unity using C#. Players solve a classic pipe-connect puzzle by twisting farm plots, uncover hidden aliens, and fight off emerging hordes. The game combines environmental puzzle-solving with first-person combat and alien encounters.

Key Implementations & Learnings
  • Developed a plot-twisting mechanic inspired by classic pipe-connect puzzles.
  • Implemented interactive farm plots, animations, and alien spawning.
  • Built first-person player controls, camera, crosshair, and shooting mechanics.
  • Developed alien AI with movement, health, damage, and death systems.
  • Designed multiple alien types with different stats and behaviors.
  • Gained practical experience with Unity C#, gameplay systems, prefab workflows, and puzzle-combat integration.

02 / Mechanics & Systems

Inverzio

An intense 3D endless runner featuring an instant gravity-flipping mechanic, real-time procedural track assembly, and dynamic difficulty scaling built from scratch in Unity.

Key Implementations & Learnings
  • Core Gravity-Flipping Mechanic: Instantly flip gravity to run on the ceiling and avoid impossible obstacles.
  • Endless Procedural Generation: The track is built in real-time, offering infinite replayability.
  • Dynamic Difficulty: As your score climbs, new and more challenging level chunks are unlocked and added to the mix.
  • Coin Collection & High Score System: Weave through obstacles to collect coins and chase a new personal best.
  • Full Audio Control: A settings menu with a persistent volume slider lets you customize your audio experience.
  • Pause System: Need a break? A fully functional pause menu has you covered.

03 / Spatial Computing

AR Treasure Hunt

The app uses level unlock system and point system. The 1st three levels are unlocked by default, player needs to clear the three levels to proceed further. The levels consist of a riddle and a hint, hint uses some points, to clear the level a person needs to solve the riddle and scan the answer in real world. Every level rewards some points.

Key Implementations & Learnings
  • Engineered a progressive multi-tier level unlock architecture with state persistence.
  • Built an in-game point economy where hints consume resources and stage completions award points.
  • Integrated real-world environmental scanning to confirm riddle answers via mobile camera feed.
  • Designed responsive spatial UI, riddle clue HUD, and 3D interactive chest prefabs.
  • Managed solo end-to-end development with Unity AR Foundation and touch gesture pipelines.
